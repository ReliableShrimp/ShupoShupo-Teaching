# The Sandbox (`/unpolished`)

Welcome to the trenches. This folder is the raw, unedited chronological proof of work. It stands as living proof that nobody writes perfect code on the first try. 

If you want to see the reality of wrestling with complex architectures, fighting tensor shape mismatches, and losing your sanity to Unicode formatting, you are in the right place.

## Expectations vs. Reality
* **Syntax Status:** Highly volatile. Code here might compile, or it might throw a runtime error that makes you question your life choices.
* **The Commit History:** A direct reflection of panic, sleep deprivation, and minor victories.
* **Typos & Grammar:** Left exactly as they were written during the initial coding sprints. 

## Rules of the Jungle
1. **Don't open PRs for typos or formatting here:** The mistakes are historical artifacts. They stay. They are part of the family. (A proof of hardwork)
2. **Use it as a reference, not a blueprint:** If you want code that doesn't melt your machine, go back to the main directory and switch to `/polished`.
3. **Appreciate the struggle:** If you are building your own track, look at this directory whenever you feel stupid. We all have our struggles, and that normal.
